Here’s how you import DICOM studies into HEYEX 2:
1.	Launch HEYEX 2 and log in with your credentials.
2.	Click the Import tab in the top action bar.
3.	Click the Drive icon (usually looks like a hard-drive symbol) — this is “Import DICOM data from a drive.”
4.	In the left-hand pane, browse to the folder or network share where your DICOM files live.
5.	In the right-hand pane, select your HEYEX 2 personal endpoint if prompted, or just stay on “Local.”
6.	Navigate down to the folder containing your study series, then click on the series (it will highlight in yellow).
7.	Choose one of the three import modes:
o	Import (just bring the images in)
o	Import and Assign (load and immediately link them to an existing patient/study)
o	Import with Worklist (associate them to a scheduled order in your RIS)
8.	Hit Import. HEYEX 2 will copy the files in, verify integrity, and show you a progress bar.
9.	When it’s done, your new DICOM study appears under the patient’s record for review, annotation, and further analysis.
